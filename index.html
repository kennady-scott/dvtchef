Our approach

Not a side gig. The whole job.

The joy of gathering friends and family in your home — or your vacation home — and entertaining them with outstanding food and incredible libations is a gift. Maybe you were skiing all day, or shopping, or doing absolutely nothing. The moment the first guest arrives and everyone begins to indulge, you’re in for a treat.

The evening is going to be a success, and your only job is to enjoy it. The moment. The conversation. The company. The food.

Everything else is handled: a menu written from scratch for your table, the shopping, the cooking, the serving — and a kitchen left cleaner than it was found.

“We are handling everything else.”

The chef

Meet Chef Drew

Chef Drew Scott has spent most of his culinary career in the Roaring Fork Valley. He started at Syzygy in 2000 under the strict guidance of Austrian chef Martin Oswald, learning the principles of fine cuisine — then went out on stages across California: Wolfgang Puck’s Postrio in San Francisco, Madrona Manor under chef Jesse Malgren, and The French Laundry.

Back home, he helped open a casual spot in Basalt, served as executive chef at Elevation until 2010, then spent nearly a decade as owner-operator of the Downvalley Tavern in El Jebel. Since 2019 he has cooked the way he likes best — for private clients, at their tables, as dvtChef.

“My true passion has always been hospitality — to nourish and feed the body and soul, and to leave my guests with an overwhelming feeling of contentment.”
