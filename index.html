Garlic Rosemary
Chicken Thighs
⏱ 30 min🔥 Easy🍽 Serves 4
The whole recipe is right here. No life story, no 45-foot scroll, and every step tells you exactly how much.

You’ll need

•6 bone-in chicken thighs
•3 cloves garlic, smashed
•2 sprigs fresh rosemary
•1 lb baby potatoes, halved
•2 tbsp olive oil
•1 tsp kosher salt · ½ tsp black pepper

Before you start

•Pat the chicken very dry — this is what gives you the crispy skin.
•Get an oven-safe skillet out. Heat the oven to 425°F.
