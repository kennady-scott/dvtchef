Our approach

Not a side gig. The whole job.

Every menu is written from scratch, for one table, once. The menu for your night doesn’t exist until Drew hears about your night — no banquet templates, no steam-table fare, nothing reheated. (Unless you order the drop-off. Then: gloriously reheatable, on purpose.)

He shops it, preps it, cooks it in your kitchen or on site, serves it, and leaves the place cleaner than he found it. For bigger events that means full setup and breakdown, coursing that lands on time, and a plan for every what-if — proactive, not reactive.

And the kitchen becomes part of the party. Guests drift in, the pans get loud, something hits the fire — the cooking is the show, and dinner shows up hot. Catering isn’t a thing dvtChef does on the side; it’s the whole job, and your event gets the attention a restaurant can’t give it.

“Mise en place for the whole evening — not just the food.”

The chef

Meet Chef Drew

Drew is a professionally trained chef who would rather cook for your table than for a dining room of strangers. The menus are built around real conversations — what you love, what the kids will actually eat, what the night is supposed to feel like.

Based in the Roaring Fork Valley, he cooks in home kitchens from Aspen to Glenwood Springs — intimate dinners, big family gatherings, and the kind of week-of-meals that makes a busy house run.

“Good food brings people together. My job is to make the night easy — great cooking shouldn’t cost you the evening with your guests.”
